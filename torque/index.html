CREDITS & HONEST NOTES: Handling is MW's modular car.js, untouched — velocity re-projection, grip/mu, slope, two-circle collisions, gear/RPM model, top gear 75 m/s (~168 MPH). Steering direction was fixed in input.js only (the base mapped left to a right turn on screen; verified, see report). Hero car is procedural (built in code, real scale) — no downloaded car model qualified. Checked and rejected in the earlier pass: Khronos CarConcept (CC BY 4.0, Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH / Eric Chadwick, 11,778,688 bytes — over the 5 MB bar, needs GLTFLoader); Khronos ToyCar (CC0, 5,422,412 bytes — over 5 MB and a toy, not a realistic car); Kenney Car Kit / Quaternius cars (CC0 — low-poly stylised, worse than the procedural car). No binary is embedded, so nothing went through a text API path. RT = real-time approximation, not hardware path tracing. Browser PBR is not console Forza — on-device FPS and look are MW's verdict.
