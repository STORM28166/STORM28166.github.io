HOW TO PLAY

AS A PROP

Blend in! Turn into objects around the map and hide before the hunters are released. Survive until the clock hits 0:00.

  • E Turn into the prop you're standing next to
  • F Drop a duplicate decoy (3 per round)
  • Q Lock / unlock rotation
  • T Taunt (+10 HP, but it's loud!)
  • Shift Sprint   Space Jump
  • Mouse wheel Camera zoom

Every 45 seconds every prop WHISTLES and gives away its rough location. Moving in front of a hunter makes them suspicious.

AS A HUNTER

You're blind while the props hide. Then find and shoot every prop before time runs out.

  • Mouse Aim   Left click Shoot
  • Right click Aim down sights
  • R Reload
  • Q Sonar pulse: nearby props ping (25s cooldown)
  • WASD Move   Shift Sprint   Space Jump

Shooting a real object costs you 5 HP. Hitting a prop heals you. Listen for whistles: the music notes show roughly where props are.

On iPad: drag the left side to move (push to the edge to sprint), drag the right side to look, and use the buttons on the right.

Esc pauses the game.The pause button is in the top-left corner. The clock is 5:00 after the hide phase. Props win if anyone is still hidden at 0:00.
